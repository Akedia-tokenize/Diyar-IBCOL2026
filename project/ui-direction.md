# DIYAR UI: Aurora institute

The website follows direction D, chosen on 9 November 2026: the serif and the calm pages of an editorial site, the colours of an aurora, and the finish of an institute.

| Owner | Owner | Amber to coral |

## Type

Fraunces for headlines and big numbers, Inter for text, IBM Plex Mono for hashes and data. The site serves all 3 itself, under the SIL Open Font License.

## Rules

1. A room colour says where you are, never whether something passed.
2. Green, red and yellow are for check results and flags only. Grades and lender decisions stay neutral.
3. A result appears only after its check has run. Nothing animates a pass that has not happened.
4. Motion is calm: numbers count up once, the headline word turns, light moves along the evidence flow. With reduced motion, everything stays still.
5. Every page passes WCAG AA contrast in both themes and never scrolls sideways on a phone.
