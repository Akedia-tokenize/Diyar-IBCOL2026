Same one-sided quarterly test as `src/e3a_quarterly.py`, with new seed 20261106 and 1,000 runs per size:

| Under-report | 20% | 22.5% | 25% | 27.5% | 30% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Detected | 67.7% | 75.7% | **80.7%** | 85.0% | 89.9% |

- 80% detection is therefore reached at about a 25% under-report.
