Deploy the anchor and anchor the 91 signed days

Two transactions from your own wallet: deploy DiyarAnchor, then anchor the reference run's 91 daily roots in one call. Sepolia test ether only, no real money. MetaMask signs; this page never sees a key and makes no network request of its own.

Step 1
Connect MetaMask on Sepolia
The page asks MetaMask to switch to Sepolia (chain 11155111) and refuses to send anything on any other chain.
Not connected.
Step 2
Deploy DiyarAnchor
Init code 1,789 bytes, compiled from DiyarAnchor.sol (SHA-256 f2827fe9df9d…) with solc 0.8.26. About 427,275 gas on a local EVM.
Step 3
Anchor all 91 days in one transaction
Calls anchorBatch with the days 2016-12-31 to 2017-03-31 and their signed SMT roots. The contract recomputes every entry as SHA-256(0x20 || day || root || prev), stores each root, and must end on head 0xf6e9dfa4a8…4b77dd. About 2,586,922 gas on a local EVM.

Step 4
Save the result into the site
Replace data/anchor.json in the site folder with this text, or send it to Claude. The site's Verify page then checks it against a public Sepolia node.
