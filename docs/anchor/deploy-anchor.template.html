Deploy the anchor and anchor the __DAYS__ signed days

Two transactions from your own wallet: deploy DiyarAnchor, then anchor the reference run's __DAYS__ daily roots in one call. Sepolia test ether only, no real money. MetaMask signs; this page never sees a key and makes no network request of its own.

Step 1
Connect MetaMask on Sepolia
The page asks MetaMask to switch to Sepolia (chain 11155111) and refuses to send anything on any other chain.
Not connected.
Step 2
Deploy DiyarAnchor
Init code __INITBYTES__ bytes, compiled from DiyarAnchor.sol (SHA-256 __SRCSHORT__) with solc __COMPILER__. About __GAS_DEPLOY__ gas on a local EVM.
Step 3
Anchor all __DAYS__ days in one transaction
Calls anchorBatch with the days __FIRST__ to __LAST__ and their signed SMT roots. The contract recomputes every entry as SHA-256(0x20 || day || root || prev), stores each root, and must end on head __HEADSHORT__. About __GAS_BATCH__ gas on a local EVM.
Already anchored from another browser?
Step 4
Save the result into the site
Replace data/anchor.json in the site folder with this text, or send it to Claude. The site's Verify page then checks it against a public Sepolia node.
