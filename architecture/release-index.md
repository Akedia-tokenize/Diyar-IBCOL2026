## Read these distinctions before quoting results

- **Implemented and tested** is not the same as **deployed on public infrastructure**.
- **Offline evaluation** of E2–E4 does not mean the quarterly slow gate is integrated into the live reference pipeline.
- **Static demo evidence** is not a live building measurement feed.
- **Signed source bytes** establish cryptographic integrity, not meter calibration or physical truth.
- **Published cost figures** are structured estimates under stated assumptions, not customer pricing.

The underlying reference documentation records 112 reconciled figures for the v2.7 manuscript and reports zero mismatches. It should be re-run whenever any research figure or its methodology changes.
